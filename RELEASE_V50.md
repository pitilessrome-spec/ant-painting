- [ ] Load, storage-quota, stale-tab, date/time manipulation and reconnect testing. Local cosmetic resources are not authoritative; if economy becomes competitive or transferable, implement server-authoritative transactions, reward verification and idempotency first.

Do not reset existing progress or silently grant ad rewards. No cash prizes or tradable real-world value.
