ANT CANVAS — Game Terms

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

ANT CANVAS is a free casual puzzle and colony-management game, supplied as-is. All virtual materials, regional goods, collectibles and rewards are for in-game use only; they cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred between players. The local NPC market is not a real-money marketplace. Local progress may be lost if browser storage is cleared. Verified Telegram puzzle scores, optional cloud backup and social features depend on the connected backend. Local village progress is not a verified competitive score.

Do not exploit the leaderboard, automate fake scores, or impersonate other players. No cash prizes, guaranteed earnings, or cryptocurrency payouts are offered. Optional rewarded advertisements are identified in-game. A reward requires an eligible completed playback; free gameplay and ordinary retries remain available.
