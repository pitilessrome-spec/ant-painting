ANT CANVAS iOS — Privacy (development draft)

The guest edition stores gameplay, artwork, colony and settings locally. It does not use Telegram authentication or automatic cloud synchronization. Optional native rewarded ads use Google Mobile Ads. Consent is checked before requesting ads, and this build does not request tracking authorization. Google may process data as described in its privacy policy. Ad privacy choices are accessible from Settings where available.

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data may delete progress. The development build uses official test ads unless explicitly configured otherwise. Before public release the publisher must complete the App Store privacy disclosures, support contact and review applicable consent and age requirements.
