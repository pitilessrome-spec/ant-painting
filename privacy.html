ANT CANVAS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

ANT CANVAS is a browser puzzle and colony-management game. In browser-only mode, game progress, artwork, village resources, worker and industry state, weekly goals, and sound preferences are saved on your own device using localStorage. We do not require an account to play.

Telegram Mini App

Once a Telegram bot and secure backend are enabled, Telegram launch data may be sent to our HTTPS server to authenticate you. We verify signed data and may store your Telegram user ID, highest verified cleared level, short-lived level-session records, and game-message context to maintain progress and a leaderboard. We also store the display name supplied in verified Telegram launch data. The leaderboard and linked friends use an anonymous label by default. You may choose to show your Telegram display name in the Scores panel and switch back to anonymous at any time. The leaderboard does not expose your full Telegram ID.

Optional cloud backup and village sharing

If you use Friends & Cloud, the service stores an explicit backup of your game, collection, and village under your verified Telegram account. Your village remains private unless you choose to publish it to invitation-linked friends. Visits expose building layouts and level, not your private save or material balances. You may disable sharing at any time. Likes, daily friendship postcards and visits are stored as account-linked events; recent visitor names follow their name-visibility preference. Weekly festival entries are optional, freeze a shared region layout for verified players, and can be withdrawn. A private village is excluded from the festival.

Ads and rewards

ANT CANVAS integrates AdsGram for optional rewarded advertisements in Telegram: fifth slot, revival, triple speed, double warehouse collection, instant building upgrade, expedition acceleration, daily bonus, and double chest materials. Ordinary retries, hints, normal speed, timed construction, standard rewards and basic gameplay remain free. If no ad is available, playback is incomplete or fails, or a reward is no longer eligible, no additional reward is granted. AdsGram may process Telegram and device or ad-interaction information under its own privacy practices. See AdsGram documentation. Session-only ad diagnostics are not revenue records; the publisher uses AdsGram reporting for earnings.

Data choices

You can clear local progress using browser settings. The game's official entry is @AntCanvasArtBot. An independently verified publisher support and account-deletion channel is still a release requirement; the bot link is not a guarantee that a deletion request will be processed. We do not sell game progress data.
