ANT CANVAS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

ANT CANVAS is a browser puzzle game. In browser-only mode, your current level and sound preference are saved on your own device using localStorage. We do not require an account to play.

Telegram Mini App

Once a Telegram bot and secure backend are enabled, Telegram launch data may be sent to our HTTPS server to authenticate you. We verify signed data and may store your Telegram user ID, highest verified cleared level, short-lived level-session records, and game-message context to maintain progress and a leaderboard. The public leaderboard displays an anonymous player label, not your Telegram account name.

Optional cloud backup and village sharing

If you use Friends & Cloud, the service stores an explicit backup of your game, collection, and village under your verified Telegram account. Your village remains private unless you choose to publish it to invitation-linked friends. Visits expose building layouts and level, not your private save or material balances. You may disable sharing at any time.

Ads and rewards

ANT CANVAS integrates AdsGram for an optional rewarded advertisement to unlock a fifth slot for the current level attempt in Telegram. Standard gameplay, hints, speed and revivals do not require an advertisement. If no ad is available or playback fails, no ad-slot reward is granted. AdsGram may process Telegram and device or ad-interaction information under its own privacy practices. See AdsGram documentation. Session-only ad diagnostics are not revenue records; the publisher uses AdsGram reporting for earnings.

Data choices

You can clear local progress using browser settings. To request deletion of future online progress, contact the publisher through the game's official Telegram bot once launched; a support contact must be added before production backend activation. We do not sell game progress data.
