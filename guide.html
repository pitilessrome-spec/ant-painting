🐜 ANT CANVAS

Little ants. Big art. A world to grow.

Color puzzles · Colony life · Four worlds · Collection

PlayMy world中文玩法

1. Color puzzle & artwork

Tap a colored box to send its ants into an available slot. Ants remove exposed matching blocks from the bottom up. Plan the sequence so slots do not fill with blocked colors. Clear all blocks to reveal art and earn building materials on first completion. Every fifth level earns a chest. Retry freely; optional rewarded ads unlock a fifth slot, revival or 3× speed.

2. Your ant civilization

Start with five ants. Assign wood gatherers, gardeners, builders or scouts. Tap numbered work sites to dispatch ants; delivery points receive materials after complete 60-second trips. Collect whole materials from storage. Upgrade the queen from level 1 to 5 to raise capacity from 5 to 13 ants, hatch a new worker in three minutes, and upgrade the warehouse from 8 to 24 hours of production storage. Build and upgrade village landmarks for production bonuses.

Each region has its own inventory. Travel to the destination to use delivered goods. One caravan at a time; workshops run separately by region. Exchange up to three processed goods per local day at the NPC market. No real-money trading.

4. Explore, collect & return

Discover relics on expeditions, build region mastery, collect paintings and chest souvenirs, unlock building themes, sign in daily, complete colony achievements and weekly goals. Claim three weekly tasks to earn a bonus chest. Telegram players can opt into cloud backup, friend visits, postcards, likes and weekly decoration festivals through Friends & Cloud.

Optional AdsGram rewards include revive, fifth slot, 3× speed, double warehouse materials, finish building upgrade, halve expedition time, daily bonus and double chest materials. Ads may be unavailable; ordinary gameplay remains free.

5. Special puzzle levels & mastery

Every third level rotates Rainbow Rush (more frequent color blasts with Hybrid Ants enabled), Precision Puzzle (different box queue layout), and Ant Sprint (smaller boxes, faster ant dispatch). A normal clear still unlocks the next painting. Earn up to three stars: two for at least 65% productive box dispatches; three for at least 85% with no hints, reshuffle, revive or extra slot. A productive dispatch clears at least one cell immediately. Stars are local cosmetic records and never change the material economy.

6. Special ants and daily puzzles

Ice blocks thaw by clearing the block immediately below. Bomb and wild powers appear on selected levels, charge after three productive dispatches, and work once when ants finish their current work. Bomb clears up to five connected nearby cells; wild ants clear up to six reachable cells of any color. Remaining boxes are rebuilt to match the board. Boss levels have a central marked group whose HP equals remaining cells. Clear the gem and finish the level to collect it. Daily uses a rotating local puzzle, saves a local medal only, and does not change campaign progress or material rewards.

7. Tactical boss shields & planning

Boss cells are locked by lower guard cells. Clear the guards to reveal the upper layers; Web Guardian uses nearby guards, Frost Sentinel uses the guard in the same column, Iron Beetle uses the full lower layer; Rainbow King requires the matching and mirrored guards. Plan Move previews an exposed color box, how many cells it clears, how many ants remain waiting, eventual free slots and unlocked special cells. Tap the same box again to commit. On bomb levels, charge and tap Bomb, then choose a highlighted reachable target. Ordinary play remains free and untimed. Gold mastery on precision and boss levels additionally requires at least 85% of dispatched boxes to be fully assigned immediately, not just one matching cell.

中文版 · 游戏玩法

核心玩法：颜色消除闯关、解锁艺术画、发展蚂蚁家园、培养蚁群、经营四大领地、运输加工与收藏探险。

先点击颜色方块，让蚂蚁清除对应颜色；通关获得木材和花瓣。进入领地分配工蚁职业，升级蚁后与仓库、孵化新蚂蚁。花园种花、森林培育发光植物、群岛航海收集贝壳、雪谷采冰晶与制作冰雕。各领地货物通过商队运输，再由工坊加工成产品，向游戏内商店兑换建设材料。

完成每日签到、任务、每周目标与成就，解锁绘画、遗物、宝箱及建筑主题。通过 Telegram 好友与云端入口体验备份、互访及装饰比赛。正常闯关、采集、孵化和建设免费，激励广告为可选额外奖励。

特殊关卡：每三关轮换彩虹狂欢、精密调度、蚂蚁冲刺。有效派遣指选择色盒后立即产生消除；65%获得两星，85%且不用提示、重排、复活和额外槽获得三星。星级为本地荣誉，不影响通关及材料。

特殊蚂蚁与每日挑战：冰冻格必须先清除下方格子；炸弹与万能蚂蚁在指定关卡中通过三次有效派遣充能，每关可使用一次，自动重新整理剩余色盒。BOSS 关清除带标记的目标格即可降低血量；宝石随通关保存。每日挑战按 UTC 日期轮换本地关卡，只记录奖章，不改变主线进度或发放材料。

存档：普通浏览器为本地存档，跨设备需要主动备份和恢复。当前经济进度不是服务端认证的竞技成绩。
